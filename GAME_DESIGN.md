# The Forgotten Island · Game design

## Premise

Arrive on a quiet tropical island and retrace a 1986 surveying expedition. The team adapted nineteenth-century coastal signal ruins into a tide and weather observatory. A surveyor's archive reveals why the island vanished from official records. The world and all discoveries are original and grounded in engineering and history.

## Map and route

The ground footprint spans roughly 500 × 500 m. North is negative Z. A sandy south landing connects to a divided palm trail. A western spur climbs through the survey camp to the hidden falls. An old inland trail reaches the signal ruins, then forks to the north-eastern tower and east cliffs. A coast trail descends to the sea-level archive. The trail network forms a loop back to the southern side. Rock shoulders and vegetation hide four optional detours.

| Place | Landmark | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Arrival Beach | Wreck and weathered dock | Learn movement and examine the expedition's landing log |
| Palm Forest | Split trail and tall palms | Choose a route, discover a short hidden path |
| Expedition Camp | Canvas shelter and survey table | Compare a departure record with numbered entries, open the lockbox |
| Hidden Waterfall | Basalt terraces and turquoise pool | Recover a bearing clue and a spare lamp bulb |
| Ancient Ruins | Stone arcade and orange signal mast | Trace historic signals, operate the relay |
| Observation Tower | Timber stairs, amber roof and sighting scope | Repair the scope and verify a genuine landscape alignment |
| Rocky Coast | Painted markers, pocket beach and split rocks | Apply a bearing clue and locate the archive |
| Research Chamber | Steel door and preserved instruments | Set the tide lock and read what happened to the expedition |

## Main sequence

Inspect the wreck → read the landing note → find camp → deduce a numeric code → recover the lens → find the falls → read the keeper's survey → fire the ruins relay → reach the tower → restore and align the scope → apply the coast bearing and open the archive → read its final dispatch.

The terrain remains freely explorable. Evidence is retained regardless of collection order. The sole physical story gate is the archive. The lockbox uses number entry, the relief uses an ordered input, the scope requires placement of two differently shaped components, its sight uses visual alignment, the coast uses a map bearing and the archive uses a tide selector. Wrong answers allow immediate retry.

## Secondary exploration and art

Five views, four concealed spots and ten optional survey artifacts add reasons to leave the trail. The ending keeps the island open. Warm slate and terracotta structures contrast with layered blue water and deep green palms. All structures, trails, sky, foliage and sound are made for the game with procedural geometry and audio. Per-session settings include sensitivity, field of view, graphics and sound. The game’s interface and narrative default to Romanian, with English available in Settings.

## Release 1.4

The scope changes the active 3D camera while the player stays on the observation platform. The nearer sighting board is a frame that reveals the second marker through it. The original field sheet and later official chart share the same reference marks. Laying the sheets together at the archive desk exposes the missing island and shoal before the final dispatch can be read.
